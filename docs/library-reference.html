Brief About the Library


The importance of the University library to teaching, learning and research activities cannot be overemphasized. The University Library was established in 2021 when the institution was founded at its permanent site.


Librarian’s Welcome Note
Maryam Saleh
University Librarian

Dear our esteemed readers and researchers, it is my pleasure to welcome you to Library, Huda University Gusau. We are committed towards providing the needed essential and fundamental knowledge and information for research and lifelong learning.

The Library resources and services are geared toward meeting the information needs of faculty members, students and researchers.

The University Library is a hybrid of print and e-resources with facilitating conditions for patrons to have access to variety of information resources and services. The Library Staff are always working hard to ensure that our patrons are provided with diverse access to variety of resources and services.

Thank you.

Library Opening Hours
The Library is opened from 8:00am – 9:00pm on Mondays to Fridays, and 9:00am – 4:00pm on Saturdays. The Library remains closed on Sundays and public holidays. NB: The library website (e-library) is opened and reachable round the clock and throughout globe.
