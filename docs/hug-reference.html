The University

Huda University Gusau is the first accredited private University in Zamfara State with its campus located at Eastern Bypass, Sokoto Road, Gusau.

Mission

The primary mission of the University is to create an effective world class educational system that engages and prepares all students to be globally competitive for colleges and careers.

Vision

The vision of the University is to:
a) impact society in a transformational way, regionally, nationally and globally, by engaging with partners outside the traditional bounderies of the University campus; and
b) contribute to society, through the pursuit of education, learning and research at the highest international level of excellence.

Core Values

1. Godliness;
2. Academic Excellence; and
3. Civic Responsibility.
